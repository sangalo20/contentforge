# Fabrication Audit & Provenance Tracing (Joe's content)

How to catch and fix fabricated/wrong claims in Joe's posts, and how to trace any
claim back to its real source. Born from a June 2026 incident Joe caught.

## The incident (why this exists)

Two scheduled Joe LinkedIn posts were wrong:
1. **Origin post** — "My first deal was a small facility in a market most investors
   would've skipped… the rent showed up every month." Joe: *"that's not how my first
   deal went or why I got into storage."* → **fabricated origin story.**
2. **Rent-roll post** — listed "month-to-month tenants who could leave next week" as a
   hidden risk. Joe: *"all storage tenants are month to month."* → **storage-domain
   error** (true for apartments/commercial leases, false for storage).

Both were **generated by `linkedin-posts`** (verified via session_search of the
generation session), dropped into a review doc, then pushed to Metricool. They were
NOT authored by the agency in Metricool — that was an early wrong conclusion drawn from
reading the *last* place the text appeared instead of where it originated. **Lesson:
trace a claim to its FIRST appearance, not its most recent one, before assigning cause.**

## Root cause — the real lesson Joe drew out

The no-fabrication HARD RULE existed, but only in `joe-from-podcasts`. The content that
leaked was made in `linkedin-posts` (no truth rule) and passed through `linkedinreview`
(copied source through faithfully, no audit). **A rule only guards the ONE entry point
it's written into.** Content travels multiple paths (generator → review doc → Metricool;
or agency-authored → Metricool). A truth rule must live at EVERY path, especially the
funnel everyone passes through (the review-doc build step).

Fix applied (now live): no-fabrication HARD RULE added to `linkedin-posts` (top +
NEVER-write list + checklist) AND a TRUTH + STORAGE-DOMAIN AUDIT GATE added to
`linkedinreview` (audit every piece at doc-build, any source).

Also note: the SM Content Tracker's own hook angle for the rent-roll topic (#3) literally
says "bad debt, concessions, and month-to-months" — so the wrong framing was SEEDED in the
source angle, not purely invented. The audit gate must catch domain errors even when the
upstream source contains them. (Correcting that tracker hook is a Notion write — gated on
Joe's explicit go, never done automatically.)

## The audit gate (run on EVERY piece before it reaches Joe)

For each post (long + short form), check three things:
1. **Fabrication** — every first-person claim (story/deal/memory/number/client/result/
   origin) must trace to a real source: soul.md, Notion, a transcript, a verified Joe
   fact. Plausible ≠ true. Untraceable → cut, reframe as idea-only/opinion, or mark
   inline `⚠ VERIFY: <what to confirm>` so Joe sees the flag. NEVER pass clean.
2. **Storage-domain truth** — true *for self-storage*, not just true-sounding for real
   estate. Known trap: **month-to-month is UNIVERSAL in storage = normal, never a
   risk/instability flag.**
3. **Provenance** — a guest's story/result belongs to the guest, attributed; never
   transplanted into Joe's mouth as lived experience.

If it fails: correct to a sourced version OR surface to Joe with `⚠ VERIFY`. Tell Joe
which pieces were flagged/changed and why — don't silently fix or silently pass.

## Provenance tracing — the procedure that worked

1. **Find where the claim FIRST appeared.** Use `session_search` on the distinctive
   phrase ("first deal small facility most investors would've skipped", "month-to-month
   tenants could leave"). The match shows the generation session + the skill that wrote
   it. This is how we proved it came from `linkedin-posts`, not the agency.
2. **Search all knowledge bases for a REAL source** before writing or rebuilding:
   - `notion_search` for the topic (bio, origin, track record, episode pages).
   - Read candidate Notion pages with `notion_fetch` — and **verify WHOSE story it is.**
     Trap caught: "SM Ep 001 — Christin Blunk" contains a vivid first-deal story
     (66-unit Radcliffe KY, $320K cold call, 37% below market, Rod's cancer) — but it's
     **Christin & Rod's** deal, NOT Joe's. Would have been fabrication to use for Joe.
   - For Joe's OWN story, his guest podcast appearances are the gold source. The
     **Ruben Kanya / "In The Lab"** transcript held Joe's real origin in his own words.
3. **Read Drive transcripts** (they are native Google Docs exported as `.docx`):
   - Download WITH export mime, else you get a 0-byte/non-zip file:
     `google_api.py drive download <ID> --output /c/Users/somea/x.docx --export-mime "application/vnd.openxmlformats-officedocument.wordprocessingml.document"`
   - Confirm it's a real docx: `head -c4 file.docx | xxd` → must show `504b 0304` (PK zip).
   - Extract with `C:/Python313/python.exe` + `python-docx` (system python has it; sandbox
     python does NOT). Search the joined text for keywords: "first deal", "got into",
     "got started", "my first", "1031", "background", year markers ("2003","2016").
   - Clean up temp `.docx` + extract script when done.
