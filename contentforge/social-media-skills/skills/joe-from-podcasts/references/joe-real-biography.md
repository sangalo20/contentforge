## The verified arc (Joe's actual story, his words)
1. **Financial advisor out of college.** Never loved it. **9/11 was his wake-up call** — "this truly is the world's casino… this isn't for me." Always wanted to be in real estate.
2. **Bought the bar he was bartending at** (worked it two nights/week in his 20s). Rebuilt it as a bar + restaurant. **The kitchen sank him — out of business 11 months later, lost ~$100K**, the only time he was ever on unemployment. Lesson he repeats: "you have to mind your kitchen" (know how to operate the thing) + "your employees are not owners."
3. **From Philadelphia → invokes the Rocky theme:** "it's not how hard you get knocked down, it's how you get back up."
4. Took a **radio sales job** → met a client selling real-estate education seminars → bought the course = "my first foray into real estate."
5. Another client connected him to **commercial real estate** (needed a sales guy with a securities license — he had it as a financial advisor). **Raised ~$90M for 1031-exchange syndications** (multifamily + office) through alternative-investment broker-dealers. **2008 crash pulled the rug out** (had just married, had a daughter).
6. **Reinvented into distressed debt** — charged-off residential second mortgages (with a partner, US Mortgage); became well-regarded ("you want to buy from them"). Spoke on stage at alternative-investment conferences about distressed debt.
7. **~2016/17: a cold email from SCOTT MEYERS.** Subject line: **"real estate without toilets, tenants, and trash."** The line that stopped him: **"80% of all self-storage facilities are owned by mom-and-pop."** Coming from distressed debt, he knew mom-and-pop = inefficiency = opportunity. **He'd never rented a storage unit in his life (still hasn't).** Bought Scott's ~$1,000 home-study course, **read it cover to cover twice.** That was the inflection point — a realization, not a deal.
