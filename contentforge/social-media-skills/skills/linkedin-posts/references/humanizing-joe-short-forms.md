# Humanizing Joe's short forms — worked before/after

The short forms (the X version, IG/short captions) are the repeat humanizer
failure. They get treated as "just trim the long post," which keeps the stiff AI
connective tissue intact. Joe caught this directly (June 2026): *"You are forgetting
to humanize them. Mostly the short forms."*

Long-form usually reads okay because it has room to breathe. The short form is where
the assembled-by-a-machine tells survive: em dashes, "X is…, Y is…" parallels,
"not X, but Y" tails, rule-of-three, and faintly slogan-y closes.

## What "Joe said it out loud" sounds like in short form

- Short flat **declaratives**. Fragments are fine. "Looks solid. It usually isn't."
- **No em/en dashes.** A stray dash is Joe's tell that the pass didn't happen.
- No tidy contrast scaffolding ("It's not just X, it's Y"), no rule-of-three lists,
  no "-ing" tails, no signposting/filler.
- Pull his REAL voice/phrasing forward over clever constructions ("Coming from
  distressed debt, I know that pattern cold" beats an engineered parallel).
- Read it aloud in your head. If it sounds like a marketer assembled it, rewrite.

## Worked examples (this session)

### Physical vs. economic occupancy — middle line

Before (assembled — em dash + "X is…, Y is…" parallel):
> That's physical occupancy — units with stuff in them. Economic occupancy is what's
> actually collected. A place that looks 91% full can pay like 78%.

After (Joe, flat declaratives, no dash):
> That's physical occupancy. Units with stuff in them. It says nothing about who's
> actually paying.

### Same post — close

Before (slogan-y "not X, but Y" tail):
> Underwrite what's banked, not what's billed.

After (plain instruction):
> A place that looks 91% full can collect like it's 78% full. Ask for the collections
> report before you model anything.

### Mom-and-pop opportunity — middle line

Before (filler + negation-list cadence):
> Rates frozen for years. No website, no dynamic pricing. Where there's inefficiency,
> there's opportunity.

After (his real lens pulled forward):
> Rates that haven't moved in years. Half of them run on a notebook. Coming from
> distressed debt, I know that pattern cold.

## The structural lesson (why the rule kept not sticking)

The humanizer lived in a separate skill; the content skills only *referenced* it, so
under focus the pass got skipped. Fix applied June 2026: a **HUMANIZER GATE** is now
a non-skippable checklist item placed right before the Output Format section in EVERY
content-creation skill (linkedin-posts, joe-from-podcasts, storagemoguls-podcast,
successfulidiots-podcast, joe-cta, audience-comment-replies, linkedin-engage). The
general principle Joe and the agent agreed on: **when a rule keeps failing, move it
out of "knowledge I should recall" and into "a gate that blocks output."**
